The association's bank accounts

Donations are accepted from inside Saudi Arabia only, with a Saudi mobile number and a card issued by a Saudi bank (mada, credit card or Apple Pay). An electronic receipt is issued once payment is complete. Payment is processed by the licensed Moyasar gateway, and the association does not keep card details.
