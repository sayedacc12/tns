About Us

An association born of a longing that deserves to be fulfilled

Tayseer Al-Nusuk is a non-profit civil association under establishment, headquartered in Riyadh. It is founded by a team of volunteers experienced in the non-profit sector and civil associations.

More about us

A complete journey From the beneficiary’s home until their return: permits, transport, accommodation and escort.

Our Vision

Leadership in facilitating the rites and elevating service to the Guests of Al-Rahman.

Our Mission

Facilitating the performance of the rites and serving the Guests of Al-Rahman through effective partnerships, distinctive programs, capacity building, and supporting initiatives and expertise.

Our Programs

Programs Serving the Guests of Al-Rahman

From facilitating the journey to training volunteers and conducting studies, our programs work together to deliver the service to those who deserve it, with quality and safety.

Umrah Program

The Umrah of a lifetime.. for those whom life’s circumstances held back

Organized Umrah trips all year round, including permits, transport, accommodation, meals and escort.

Learn more Introductory kit

“Hajjat Al-Wafa” Initiative

Giving back to those who served the nation

An honorary Hajj sponsorship for deserving groups of citizens, such as families of martyrs and members of Civil Defense and the National Guard.

Learn more Introductory kit

Hajj Program

A long-awaited pillar.. we help the deserving perform it

Sponsoring the obligatory Hajj for those who cannot afford it and have never performed it, through domestic pilgrim packages with licensed companies, with care and follow-up.

Learn more Introductory kit

Awareness & Preparation Program

Correct rites.. and a safe journey

Guiding beneficiaries on the rulings of the rites, safety and health before and during the journey.

Learn more Introductory kit

“Tayseer Academy for Volunteer Training”

A qualified volunteer.. a finer service for the Guests of Al-Rahman

Training volunteer and working staff of both genders to serve in Hajj and Umrah activities, using accredited training kits.

Learn more Introductory kit

“Tayseer Observatory for Studies”

Knowledge that informs service decisions

Research, studies and advisory expertise supporting Hajj and Umrah activities in the Kingdom, empowering service providers.

Learn more Introductory kit
Why the association?

The need in numbers

The desire to perform the rites from within the Kingdom is growing, while opportunities are shrinking and costs are rising for low-income families.

160,646domestic pilgrims in 1447 AH, only 9.4% of all pilgrims

1.7 millionpeople aged 60 and over
