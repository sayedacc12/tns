Partnerships

Our partners in impact

We believe the greatest impact comes from working together, and we welcome partnership with:

Civil foundations and endowments

Cooperation in delivering distinctive programs, with performance indicators and regular impact reports.

Corporate social responsibility

Contributing expertise and services to the programs, with media recognition and an impact report.

Government bodies

Working with bodies concerned with serving the Guests of Al-Rahman and social development.

Existing associations

Cooperation with specialized associations in nomination, delivery and exchange of expertise.

Hajj and Umrah companies

Delivery partnerships with licensed companies in transport, accommodation and packages.

Universities and research centers

Knowledge partnerships with “Tayseer Observatory for Studies”.

Our commitments

What does our partner get?

  • Regular reports on the delivery of the supported program and its performance indicators.
  • Sound governance with approved, published regulations and policies.
  • Media recognition of the partner in programs and trips, as they wish.
  • Protecting beneficiaries’ data and dignity in everything published.
