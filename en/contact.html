Frequently asked questions

Quick answers

Is the association licensed?

The association is under establishment, and its registration with the National Center for Non-Profit Sector is being completed.

Who can benefit from the association’s programs?

Saudis and residents who cannot afford it, first among them low-income families, senior citizens and people with disabilities, according to published eligibility criteria.

How do I nominate someone?

You can contact us through the form. Nominations and applications will open on the website after licensing.

Where can I see the association’s regulations and policies?

All approved regulations and policies are published on the Governance & Transparency page and available for download (in Arabic).

A non-profit civil association under establishment, headquartered in Riyadh, dedicated to enabling Saudis and residents who cannot afford it to perform Hajj and Umrah.
