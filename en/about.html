About the association

Tayseer Al-Nusuk

A non-profit civil association under establishment, headquartered in Riyadh. It aims to enable Saudis and residents who cannot afford it to perform Hajj and Umrah, through trusted programs, effective partnerships and sustainable solutions.

It is founded by a team of volunteers experienced in the non-profit sector and civil associations, and its operational team works as unpaid volunteers in the first year, so that support goes to the beneficiaries.

Our Vision

Leadership in facilitating the rites and elevating service to the Guests of Al-Rahman.

Our Mission

Facilitating the performance of the rites and serving the Guests of Al-Rahman through effective partnerships, distinctive programs, capacity building, and supporting initiatives and expertise.

Our Values

Six values that guide our work

Sincerity

We serve the Guests of Al-Rahman as an act of worship, seeking reward from Allah.

Dignity

We protect the dignity and privacy of beneficiaries at every stage.

Integrity & Transparency

We disclose our work and its results to the community and supervisory bodies.

Excellence

A safe, high-quality service from registration until return.

Partnership

We create the greatest impact by working with bodies active in the field.

Compliance

Full commitment to the relevant laws and regulations.

Our Goals

The association’s goals

As set out in the association’s Articles of Association.

Goal 1

Helping and honoring wayfarers and stranded Guests of Al-Rahman.

Goal 2

Supporting programs that facilitate the performance of the rites.

Goal 3

Partnering with bodies that serve pilgrims, Umrah performers and visitors.

Goal 4

Providing advisory expertise and research studies to empower service providers.

Goal 5

Qualifying and training volunteer and working staff of both genders.

Business Model

How the association creates and delivers value

A non-profit model: the beneficiary pays nothing, and the association multiplies the impact of its resources through partnerships and volunteering.

Beneficiary and partner segments

  • Low-income families, senior citizens, people with disabilities, honorary groups, and stranded Guests of Al-Rahman
  • Trainees and Hajj and Umrah service providers
  • Partners and supporting bodies

Value proposition

  • For the beneficiary: a complete, free and safe pilgrimage that preserves their dignity
  • For the partner: measurable impact and regular disclosure
  • For the private sector: meeting social responsibility targets
  • For the Hajj and Umrah system: qualified staff and applied knowledge

Channels

  • The website and the applications system
  • Nominating bodies and civil associations
  • The association’s official accounts

Relationships with beneficiaries and partners

  • Accompanying the beneficiary from registration until return
  • Regular impact reports for partners
  • Recognition programs for volunteers and partners

Key activities

  • Case study and eligibility verification
  • Contracting and organizing trips
  • Preparation and awareness
  • Training and qualifying staff
  • Research, studies and consulting
  • Impact measurement and disclosure

Key resources

  • An experienced volunteer founding team
  • A base of qualified volunteers
  • Digital systems and approved governance

Key partners

  • The Ministry of Hajj and Umrah and the Nusuk platform
  • The National Center for Non-Profit Sector
  • Licensed Hajj and Umrah companies
  • Nominating bodies for “Hajjat Al-Wafa”
  • Academic and research bodies

Cost structure

  • Umrah and Hajj programs (transport, accommodation, meals and escort)
  • Preparation, training and studies
  • Operations, governance and systems

Sources of resources

  • Grants and support from donor bodies and endowments
  • Social responsibility partnerships
  • Discounts earned from partners
  • The value of volunteer work
Added Value

The added value of the association’s work

The value of our work is not limited to those who perform the rites; it extends to their families, partners, the sector and society.

For beneficiaries and their families

Performing a pillar of Islam, or a long-awaited Umrah, without financial burden, through a safe service that cares for senior citizens and people with disabilities, with a psychological and social impact that extends to the family.

For honorary groups

A practical expression of society’s appreciation for those who served the nation, complementing existing government programs and reaching groups they do not cover.

For partners and supporting bodies

A trusted channel for charitable work with clear spiritual and social impact, with regular impact reports.

For the private sector

Meeting social responsibility targets, and using unused capacity at Hajj and Umrah companies outside peak times.

For the Hajj and Umrah system

Professionally qualified volunteer and working staff of both genders, and studies and advisory expertise that empower service providers and support decisions.

For the non-profit sector

A specialized, replicable model, and data on the affordability gap in performing the rites that was not available before.

For the nation and Vision 2030

Contributing to the targets of the Pilgrim Experience Program, empowering the non-profit sector and increasing the number of volunteers.
