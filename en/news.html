Coming soon: applications open for the Hajj season of 1448 AH

Tayseer Al-Nusuk announces that applications for its programs in the Hajj season of 1448 AH will open soon. The date and conditions will be announced once the official license is issued.

Tayseer Al-Nusuk is preparing to open applications for its programs in the Hajj season of 1448 AH, to serve Saudis and residents who cannot afford Hajj and have not yet performed the obligation.

The opening date, eligibility conditions and nomination process will be announced on this website as soon as the association's official license is issued.

For enquiries, please reach the association through the Contact Us page.
