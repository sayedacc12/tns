Volunteer with us

Join a team that serves the Guests of Al-Rahman with sincerity and excellence, after training at “Tayseer Academy for Volunteer Training”.

Why volunteer with us?

Serving the Guests of Al-Rahman is an honor and a reward

  • The reward of serving the Guests of Al-Rahman and helping them perform their worship.
  • Accredited training at “Tayseer Academy” with a completion certificate.
  • Your volunteer hours recorded on the National Volunteering Platform.
  • Opportunities for men and women, in the field, in the office and remotely.
  • An organized work environment governed by the Volunteering & Volunteer Management Regulation.

Volunteer Charter

Tayseer Al-Nusuk Volunteer Charter

Derived from the Volunteering & Volunteer Management Regulation (R-08) and the Code of Conduct & Work Ethics (P-08).

Volunteer rights

  • A clear task description and suitable training.
  • A safe work environment, and injury insurance for field tasks where available.
  • Coverage of approved actual expenses related to the task, such as transport and meals on trips.
  • Recorded hours and a volunteer certificate issued through the National Volunteering Platform.
  • Appreciation and recognition according to published criteria.

Volunteer duties

  • Commitment to tasks and schedules, and notifying in advance when unable to attend.
  • Following the code of conduct and treating the Guests of Al-Rahman well, especially senior citizens and people with disabilities.
  • Keeping beneficiaries’ data confidential.
  • Not accepting any payment or gift from beneficiaries or suppliers.
  • Not speaking to the media on behalf of the association without authorization.

Full text (Arabic): Volunteering & Volunteer Management Regulation · Code of Conduct
