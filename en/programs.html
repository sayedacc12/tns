Umrah Program

The Umrah of a lifetime.. for those whom life’s circumstances held back
TimingTrips all year round
Introductory kit (PDF, Arabic)

About the program

The program gives deserving low-income families, senior citizens and people with disabilities a complete and safe Umrah trip, from their homes until their return, in cooperation with licensed transport and accommodation companies, with field escort by qualified volunteers.

Beneficiaries

Low-income families, senior citizens and people with disabilities

What the program provides

PermitsIssuing Umrah permits through the Nusuk platform
TransportAccessible buses to Makkah
Accommodation & mealsAccommodation near the Haram and meals throughout the trip
EscortSupervisors and volunteers who assist with Tawaf and Sa’i
SafetyInsurance and health follow-up throughout the trip
GuidanceAwareness of Umrah rulings before departure
“We write for them the Umrah of a lifetime”
  • A complete, safe Umrah trip at no cost to the beneficiary.
  • We reach senior citizens, people with disabilities and low-income families.
  • Licensed partners and qualified volunteer escorts from home until return.
  • An impact report showing the results of every trip.

“Hajjat Al-Wafa” Initiative

Giving back to those who served the nation
TimingEvery Hajj season
Introductory kit (PDF, Arabic)

About the program

An honorary initiative sponsoring the obligatory Hajj for deserving groups of citizens, as a practical expression of society’s loyalty to those who served the nation. It complements government programs and reaches those they do not cover.

Beneficiaries

Families of martyrs, members of Civil Defense and the National Guard, and other deserving groups who have not performed the obligatory Hajj

What the program provides

NominationNominating deserving people in coordination with the relevant bodies
PackageA domestic pilgrim package with a licensed company
TransportTransport between cities and the holy sites
CareHealth follow-up and insurance throughout the season
RecognitionAn honoring ceremony and documentation of beneficiaries’ stories
PreparationGuidance on Hajj rulings before the season
“Hajjat Al-Wafa.. they gave, and we give back”
  • An honorary Hajj sponsorship for families of martyrs, members of Civil Defense, the National Guard and others.
  • It complements existing government programs and reaches groups they do not cover.
  • Official nomination by the relevant bodies and independent verification by the association.
#Hajjat_AlWafa #Giving_Back #Loyalty_to_Our_Heroes

Hajj Program

A long-awaited pillar.. we help the deserving perform it
TimingHajj season
Introductory kit (PDF, Arabic)

About the program

The program sponsors the obligatory Hajj for those who cannot afford it and have never performed it, through domestic pilgrim packages with licensed companies, with help in registration, transport, health care and follow-up throughout the season. It starts with the “Hajjat Al-Wafa” initiative and then expands to others who cannot afford it.

Beneficiaries

Those who cannot afford it and have not performed the obligatory Hajj, first among them low-income families and senior citizens

What the program provides

RegistrationHelping the beneficiary register and book through the Nusuk platform
PackageA domestic pilgrim package with a licensed company
TransportTransport between cities and the holy sites
CareHealth follow-up and insurance throughout the season
EscortQualified supervisors and volunteers with the pilgrims
PreparationGuidance on Hajj rulings and safety before the season
“We help them fulfil the obligation.. and share in the reward”
  • Full sponsorship of the package, transport, care and follow-up.
  • Priority for low-income families and senior citizens who have never performed Hajj.
  • Delivered through licensed domestic Hajj companies and the Nusuk platform.
#Obligatory_Hajj #Tayseer_AlNusuk #Guests_of_AlRahman

Awareness & Preparation Program

Correct rites.. and a safe journey
TimingBefore every trip
Introductory kit (PDF, Arabic)

About the program

The program prepares every beneficiary before and during their trip on the rulings of the rites, safety and health, so they perform their worship with understanding and return safely, through in-person sessions and simple digital content.

Beneficiaries

All beneficiaries of the association’s programs and their companions

What the program provides

Rulings of the ritesA simple explanation of the acts of Hajj and Umrah
SafetyGuidance on crowds and heat stress
HealthHealth awareness for senior citizens and people with chronic illnesses
Digital contentShort messages and videos before the trip
SessionsAn introductory session before every trip
GuidesA printed beneficiary guide
“Before they perform Tawaf.. we teach them how”
  • Every beneficiary is prepared before their trip on the rulings of the rites, safety and health.
  • Simple guidance that considers senior citizens, people with disabilities and the concessions available to them.
  • Preparation that raises the quality of worship and protects the beneficiary from health risks.
#Rites_with_Understanding #Safety_of_the_Guests_of_AlRahman

“Tayseer Academy for Volunteer Training”

A qualified volunteer.. a finer service for the Guests of Al-Rahman
TimingCourses all year round
Introductory kit (PDF, Arabic)

About the program

The academy qualifies male and female volunteers with accredited training kits to serve the Guests of Al-Rahman. They then take part in the field in the association’s trips and in the Hajj and Umrah seasons, and their hours are recorded on the National Volunteering Platform.

Beneficiaries

Male and female volunteers, and staff serving the Guests of Al-Rahman

What the program provides

Training kitsAccredited training in serving the Guests of Al-Rahman
First aidSkills for handling emergencies
Serving senior citizensSkills for escorting the elderly and people with disabilities
Field practiceReal participation in the association’s trips
CertificatesA completion certificate and documented volunteer hours
PartnershipsCooperation with accredited training providers
“Train one volunteer.. serve a thousand guests”
  • Accredited training kits for male and female volunteers.
  • Training covers the rulings of the rites, first aid, serving senior citizens and crowd management.
  • Every qualified volunteer takes part in the field in the association’s trips and the Hajj and Umrah seasons.
#Tayseer_Academy #Volunteering_for_the_Guests_of_AlRahman

“Tayseer Observatory for Studies”

Knowledge that informs service decisions
TimingPeriodic studies
Introductory kit (PDF, Arabic)

About the program

A research unit that conducts studies supporting Hajj and Umrah activities in the Kingdom, fills gaps in published data, and puts its results in the hands of relevant bodies and field workers. Its first study is an assessment of beneficiaries’ actual needs.

Beneficiaries

Bodies serving pilgrims and Umrah performers, researchers and service providers

What the program provides

Needs studiesMeasuring beneficiaries’ actual needs
Impact measurementAssessing the impact of programs on beneficiaries
Policy papersPractical recommendations for relevant bodies
ConsultingSupporting expertise for service providers
Knowledge sharingMaking results available to those interested
Research partnershipsCooperation with universities and research centers
“We measure to facilitate.. knowledge serving the Guests of Al-Rahman”
  • A research observatory filling data gaps in serving domestic pilgrims and Umrah performers.
  • Studies on the affordability gap in performing the rites and on the needs of senior citizens and people with disabilities.
  • Partnership with academic bodies specialized in Hajj and Umrah research.
#Tayseer_Observatory #Hajj_and_Umrah_Research
